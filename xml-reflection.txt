Questions for Reflection:

1. What are the basic rules of XML you applied in this lab?

Kailangan mong sabihin na gumamit ka ng proper opening and closing tags, isang root element, at tamang nesting.


2. Why does XML need a single root element?

Dahil kailangan may isang main element na naglalaman ng lahat ng ibang elements para maging organized at well-formed ang XML document.


3. How does XML ensure interoperability?

Dahil ang XML ay gumagamit ng standard text-based format na maaaring basahin at gamitin ng iba't ibang programs at systems.
